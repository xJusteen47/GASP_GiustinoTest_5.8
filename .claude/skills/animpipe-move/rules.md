# Owner rules for this project (read at setup)

These are the owner's standing process rules; the commands enforce the first four.
1. Never move the frame of an owner key: propose and wait. A key that disappears from his scene is his question, not a fix.
2. `approve` only after the owner's explicit ok on the composite grid of the current round.
3. Fingers on the owner's Cascadeur keys win over the card; change them only on his request.
4. Snapshots before every write (automatic); restore instead of hand-repairing a card.
5. Max 2 review rounds, one variant per round, one fix per attempt, max 2 attempts per failing gate.
6. Moves are judged at 60 fps, in-game camera, with hitstop: no pop after the hitbox frames.
7. Every move starts from the upright idle; check balance and the support knee on every key.
8. Look at the six views and the elbows; a joint that bends the wrong way blocks.
9. Ask before any big pose change; describe it in character terms (forward/up/out, cm or degrees).
10. The owner writes Italian: answer him in Italian.
