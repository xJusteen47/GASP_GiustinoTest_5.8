# Kit overlord (villain): style rules

- Character: "elegant violence" (big, heavy, controlled), think of classic fighting-game bosses: little wasted motion,
  long holds, sudden release.
- "Troppo è giusto": exaggerate the anticipation and the hold, platform-fighter readability over realism.
- Hands: villain hands, closed fists on the attack frames; the thumb runs "along" the fist (approved default).
- Reference timings come from platform-fighter frame data (hitbox frames, FAF): the active frames never move.
- dair: the owner's poses at the charge and the stomp are locked; never re-pose them.
