# Sources (animation, level 1)

## Books
- Richard Williams, *The Animator's Survival Kit* (Faber, expanded ed. 2009): timing and spacing charts, breakdowns,
  weight, walks and runs, successive breaking of joints, overlap, anticipation.
- Frank Thomas & Ollie Johnston, *The Illusion of Life: Disney Animation* (1981): the twelve principles.
- Jonathan Cooper, *Game Anim: Video Game Animation Explained* (CRC Press, 2nd ed. 2021): game-specific constraints,
  responsiveness, root motion, blending, readability.

## Courses (general references only)
Body-mechanics curricula (Animation Mentor, iAnimate, AnimSchool) are cited as the common practice they teach. Notes
taken from licensed course videos are **not** part of this repository; they stay in the licensee's private project
notes. A principle learned there enters level 1 only rephrased, generalised and backed by a book, a public source or a
measurement in this folder.
