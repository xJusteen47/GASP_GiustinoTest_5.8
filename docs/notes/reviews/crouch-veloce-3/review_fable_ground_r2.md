# Review Fable r2: fast ground crouch (crouch-veloce-3, capture v5)

**Verdict: FAIL** (narrow). Timing and follow-through pass; the held pose is better but still a one-knee kneel, and from the side it reads as "the thinker".

Seen (7 images): v5 side / front34 / game grids, v3 side grid, and three composites of single frames: az90 f040-f048, az90 f110-f126, f070 in six views. Not seen: the mp4. Frame counts are +/- 1.

## Identity traits
1. **Fast in/out: PASS.** Enter: f040 standing, f041 first bend, f043 deep lunge, f045 lowest point (overshoot), f048 settled: 4 frames down, settle about 8. Exit: f112 still kneeling, f113 almost standing, f114 up: about 3 frames, a bit under the 4-5 target and badly spaced (see below).
2. **Follow-through: PASS.** Enter: arms hang behind at f042-f043, fists land on the knee at f044-f045, rise to the guard at f046-f048; head dips at f045 and lifts at f046. Exit: head tilts back at f113 (lag), forearms still forward at f114, down at f115, small chest settle f114-f118. The new exit overshoot is visible.
3. **Style, guarded crouch: FAIL (improved).** Better than v3: pelvis is off the heel, chest is more upright, fists are up; from the front (az0 f070) it reads as a boxing guard. Still wrong: back knee, shin and the top of the back foot lie flat on the floor (resting kneel, not ready to spring); the chin fist touches the chin with the elbow on the body line, so the side view (az90 f070) is "the thinker"; elbows are tucked, so the game camera (game f070) shows a ball with no arms.

## FAIL items
- **posa, f048-f110 (back leg).** Round 1 item not applied. Lift the back knee 10-12 cm off the floor, back foot on the ball with the heel up (toes bent, not the top of the foot on the floor), weight shared 50/50. Keep the pelvis height you have now.
- **posa, f048-f110 (arms).** Chin fist: move it 12-15 cm in front of the chin and 5 cm below it (open the elbow about 20 deg), never touching the face. Both elbows out: upper arm roll from 15 to about 35 deg, so the forearms show outside the torso from behind. Head 6-8 deg more chin-up: in az90 the face still points at the floor.
- **posa, check (inferred from az0 only).** The raised fist looks like the right one while the front leg is the left. If so, swap: lead fist on the front-leg side.
- **dati montage, f112-f114 (exit spacing).** About 80% of the rise happens in one frame (f112 to f113), from knee on the floor to standing: a pop. Spread it linearly over 4 frames (25% per frame), then the overshoot. Lifting the back knee in the held pose also shortens this travel.
- **gioco, f041 and f111 (camera), not addressed as agreed.** The horizon still jumps in one frame with the capsule at press and release. Same fix as round 1.
- **gioco, f116-f142 (exit stance), not addressed as agreed.** The stride is still held to about f138 and the feet come together at f142. Same fix as round 1.

## Keep
Enter timing and the linear drop (no tall stride at f042 any more), front foot planted, the f045 overshoot, arm lag on enter, exit overshoot and head lag, pelvis height, chest angle.
