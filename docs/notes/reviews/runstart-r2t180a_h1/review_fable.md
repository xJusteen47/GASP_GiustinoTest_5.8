## Identity traits
1. Weight transfer into the new direction
   - 180: PASS. Round 1 backward tip is gone. v088 sinks upright (knees bent, torso vertical), v092 chest and head are
     clearly ahead of the hips (az0: shoulders twisted, head leading), v096 the body banks ~40 deg along the chest (sideways
     in az0, mild in az90), v100-v108 it falls ~35-40 deg toward the run direction with the head low and in front of the
     feet (az90). No frame with the head behind the support. Head height drops steadily v088 -> v096, no bob.
     180 R shows the same order (v096-v108 hunch forward, v108 ~40 deg into the run).
   - 90: PASS. v088 already sunk and pitched ~35 deg on the diagonal between old and new heading, v096-v112 low and
     driving to the new heading (az0), well before stock (stock still upright at v092-v096).
   - Both: torso is back to ~15-20 deg by v116-v124, while stock holds ~40 deg until v128. This is the approved w2
     behaviour (rise on the long stride), so not a fail, but the low phase is short: about v096-v112.
2. Frantic limbs, no planted pause: PASS for both. Feet are on the toes and stepping from v088, no flat planted pivot
   (stock pivots on a wide planted stance v096-v108). Arms pump from v088; on the R clips the elbows flare wide at v088-v092.
3. No clash, no backward knees: PASS on what stills show. v136-v148 ours and stock are almost the same pose in all views,
   no visible hop or double step. No backward knee seen (straightest leg: front leg at v128, az90 180 L, still fine).

## Life
- Anticipation: good on the 180 (sink + head/chest wind at v088-v092 reads before the body goes). On the 90 there is
  almost none: idle at v084, full crouch and pitch at v088. Reads snappy and cartoony, but it is the place to check for a pop.
- Arc: the 180 now has one clear arc (down, around, forward). The bank at v096 is a nice "motorbike" moment.
- Weight: weakest point is the base. 180 L v096-v100 the legs are together under the pelvis while the torso is 40 deg off
  (az90 v100 = one straight diagonal plank, poor silhouette). Stock sells weight with a wide catch step here; ours does not.
- Follow-through: arms and head settle cleanly into the loop by v136; nothing drags or overshoots (a little overshoot of
  the head at v108-v112 would add life).
