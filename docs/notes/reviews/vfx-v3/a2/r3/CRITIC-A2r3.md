# Blind critic: landing dust A2r3

First impression, dark floor: a handful of faint, bluish, soft puffs around the feet. They read as cold breath or steam blobs more than kicked-up dust.
First impression, light floor: small grey-brown puffs that are easy to see but darker and dirtier than the floor. They read as smoke or soot more than dust.

## Dark floor (A2r3)
- Reads as: separate round ghost puffs (4-5 clumps with gaps), not one billow (zoom_dark_f166 top; consec_dark f162-f168). Bluish light halo with a brighter core looks a little like glow or steam (zoom_dark_f166, left and far-right puffs).
- Colour vs floor: lighter navy-lavender, close in hue. Slightly too blue and too luminous for dust (consec_dark f160-f166).
- Softness: soft and blurred through f168, much softer than A2r2's crunchy cauliflower (zoom_dark_f166 top vs bottom). From f170-f173 the clumps pick up dark noisy blotches and look crunchy again (consec_dark f170-f172; game_dark A2r3 f170).
- Height: stays at ankle height, well below the knee (side_dark A2r3 f160-f170). It never covers the character.
- Timing/fade: puffs are already visible at f156-f157, before the touchdown at f158 (consec_dark f156; game_dark A2r3 f157). Peak is around f162-f166. Clumps shrink rather than spread, and one lone puff on the right lingers to f176-f179 (consec_dark f176-f179) after the rest are gone.
- Pops/shapes: no rectangles, lines or rings. White pebble specks near the left foot at f162-f170 read as sparks against navy (consec_dark f162, f164; zoom_dark_f166).
- Readability: low. At game distance it is barely there (game_dark A2r3 f160-f167 vs OFF).

## Light floor (A2r3)
- Reads as: grey-brown smoke or soot puffs (zoom_light_f166 top, right clump; consec_light f159-f163). Better presence than A2r2, which was almost invisible (zoom_light_f166 bottom).
- Colour vs floor: clearly darker and warmer (brown-grey) than the near-white floor. Dust from this floor should be pale and only slightly shaded (zoom_light_f166 top; side_light A2r3 f160-f162).
- Softness: edges are soft. The cores show a darker, mottled texture that reads as dirty smoke (zoom_light_f166 right clump; consec_light f161-f162).
- Height: below the knee, roughly at shin level at peak (side_light A2r3 f162-f164). It never covers the character.
- Timing/fade: puffs appear at f156-f157, before contact (consec_light f156, f157). They fade nicely to pale by f170-f174 and are nearly gone by f178 (consec_light f174-f178; game_light A2r3 f174-f178). The later fade is the best part.
- Pops/shapes: no rectangles or rings from the dust. A grey ellipse under the feet comes and goes from frame to frame (consec_light f158, f164 vs f160, f170; f167 thin as a line). It is also in the OFF row (game_light OFF), so it is likely a blob shadow and not this effect. It still flickers.

## 3 worst problems (ranked)
1. Light floor colour: the dust is darker and browner than the floor, so it reads as soot or smoke, not floor dust (zoom_light_f166 top; consec_light f159-f163).
2. Shape on both floors: separate round clumps that shrink instead of merging and spreading outward. On dark they also carry a blue glow that reads as steam (zoom_dark_f166 top; consec_dark f162-f172).
3. Timing: dust is visible at f156-f157, before the f158 touchdown, on both floors. On dark it ends with crunchy blotches (f170-f173) and a lone lingering puff (f176-f179) (consec_dark, consec_light).

## Verdict
- Dark floor: NOT YET (soft and low, but faint, blobby and steam-like).
- Light floor: NOT YET (readable and fades well, but the wrong colour: smoke, not dust).
