## Q1. In UE 5.9 with GASP, what is the recommended way to add controller haptics/force feedback on attacks and on footsteps (Xbox pad now, DualSense/Switch later), data-driven?

{"conversationId":"01M7ZDM61G77NTHR38TGW2JTJN","reply":"## Architettura consigliata\n\nIl punto chiave è separare la *sorgente dell'evento* (anim notify, Gameplay Cue) dal *contenuto dell'effetto* (asset `UForceFeedbackEffect` con override per dispositivo). Così il codice resta uguale per tutte le piattaforme e cambiano solo i dati.\n\n| Livello | Classe / asset | Ruolo |\n|---|---|---|\n| Effetto | `UForceFeedbackEffect` (**Add > Input > Force Feedback Effect**) | Curve per canale (motori Left/Right Large/Small) |\n| Variante per piattaforma | **Per Device Overrides** sullo stesso asset | Xbox ora, DualSense/Switch dopo, senza toccare il codice |\n| Funzioni extra del pad | **Device Properties** sull'asset (`UInputDeviceProperty`) | Trigger Feedback/Resistance/Vibration, colore, vibrazione da audio |\n| Riproduzione | `APlayerController::ClientPlayForceFeedback` | Solo sul controller locale |\n| Attacchi | `UGameplayCueNotify_Burst` (GAS) | Burst Force Feedback e Burst Device Property Effect |\n| Passi | `UAnimNotify` custom con tag + Data Asset | Rumble cosmetico e locale |\n\n## 1. Asset di effetto (dati)\n\n- Crea un asset per ogni \"intenzione\": `FFE_Step_Walk`, `FFE_Step_Run`, `FFE_Land_Heavy`, `FFE_Attack_Light`, `FFE_Attack_Heavy`, `FFE_HitConfirm`.\n- Passi: usa **Affects Left Large/Small** per il piede sinistro e **Right** per il destro (asset `_L` e `_R`). Le curve devono essere brevi (circa 60 ms). La durata dell'effetto viene dall'ultima chiave della curva.\n- **Per Device Overrides**: le chiavi sono gli identificatori hardware elencati in **Project Settings > Input > Platform Settings > (Platform) > Hardware**, per esempio DualSense o DualShock4 su PS5.\n- **Device Properties**, per il DualSense in seguito:\n  - **Trigger Resistance** e **Trigger Feedback** per armi pesanti o cariche.\n  - **Trigger Vibration** sull'impatto.\n  - **Audio Based Vibration** (sperimentale, solo DualSense) richiede in config:\n    ```ini\n    [SonyController]\n    VibrationMode=Advanced\n    ```\n- Switch: le specifiche dell'HD Rumble sono sotto NDA e non sono nella documentazione pubblica. Usa la documentazione della piattaforma e un override per dispositivo.\n\n## 2. Tabella dati guidata da tag\n\nCrea un `UPrimaryDataAsset` di progetto, per esempio `UHapticsProfile`, con questi dati:\n\n```cpp\nUSTRUCT(BlueprintType)\nstruct FHapticsEntry\n{\n    GENERATED_BODY()\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere) TSoftObjectPtr<UForceFeedbackEffect> Effect;\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere) float MinInterval = 0.12f; // anti-spam\n};\n\nUCLASS()\nclass UHapticsProfile : public UPrimaryDataAsset\n{\n    GENERATED_BODY()\npublic:\n    // es. Haptics.Step.Run.L, Haptics.Step.Walk.R, Haptics.Land.Heavy\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere) TMap<FGameplayTag, FHapticsEntry> Entries;\n    // opzionale: override per superficie\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere) TMap<TEnumAsByte<EPhysicalSurface>, FGameplayTag> SurfaceSuffix;\n};\n```\n\nL'intensità varia scegliendo *asset diversi* per andatura e superficie. `FForceFeedbackParameters` (Tag, Looping, ecc.) non ha un parametro di intensità per singola chiamata.\n\n## 3. Passi (AnimBP con Motion Matching)\n\nCrea un `UAnimNotify` custom, per esempio `UAnimNotify_Haptic`, con un `FGameplayTag HapticTag`. Puoi anche riusare il notify di foley tag-based che il tuo progetto GASP usa già e aggiungere lì l'hook aptico.\n\n```cpp\nvoid UAnimNotify_Haptic::Notify(USkeletalMeshComponent* Mesh, UAnimSequenceBase* Anim,\n                                const FAnimNotifyEventReference& Ref)\n{\n    APawn* Pawn = Cast<APawn>(Mesh ? Mesh->GetOwner() : nullptr);\n    if (!Pawn || !Pawn->IsLocallyControlled()) return;          // solo client locale\n    if (APlayerController* PC = Cast<APlayerController>(Pawn->GetController()))\n    {\n        // lookup nel UHapticsProfile (es. da un component sul pawn) + cooldown MinInterval\n        if (UForceFeedbackEffect* FX = /* lookup */ nullptr)\n        {\n            FForceFeedbackParameters P;\n            P.Tag = HapticTag.GetTagName(); // stesso tag = sostituisce l'effetto precedente\n            PC->ClientPlayForceFeedback(FX, P);\n        }\n    }\n}\n```\n\nImpostazioni obbligatorie per Motion Matching e blend stack:\n- Sul nodo **Motion Matching** abilita **Should Filter Notifies** e parti da **Notify Recency Time Out** = `0.2`. Senza questo filtro le pose in blend fanno partire lo stesso notify su frame adiacenti, quindi senti un doppio rumble.\n- Sui notify da non filtrare mai (per esempio impatti di montage) disattiva **Can Be Filtered Via Request**.\n- Il filtro funziona solo con gli Anim Notify, *non* con gli Anim Notify State. Per i passi usa quindi notify semplici.\n- Tieni comunque un cooldown nel componente (`MinInterval`), perché anche i blend dei Chooser possono sovrapporre gli asset.\n\n## 4. Mover + Network Prediction (62.5 Hz)\n\n- Considera il rumble dei passi come **puramente cosmetico** e guidato dall'animazione. Gli anim notify scattano durante il tick dell'animazione, non durante il tick fisso della simulazione Mover, quindi rollback e resimulazione non li rieseguono. Questo è il comportamento corretto per l'aptica.\n- *Non* generare eventi aptici dentro la simulazione Mover (per esempio nei movement mode o nelle transizioni): in resimulazione si duplicherebbero.\n- Il filtro `IsLocallyControlled()` evita che i proxy simulati facciano vibrare il pad del giocatore locale.\n- `a.EnableQueuedAnimEventsOnServer` riguarda solo i notify su listen server. Per l'aptica locale non serve.\n\n## 5. Attacchi (GAS)\n\n- Usa un `UGameplayCueNotify_Burst` per ogni tag, per esempio `GameplayCue.Haptics.Attack.Heavy`:\n  - **GCN Effects > Burst Effects > Burst Force Feedback** contiene l'asset `FFE_Attack_*`.\n  - **Burst Device Property Effect > Device Properties** contiene la resistenza o vibrazione del grilletto per il DualSense.\n  - Imposta la spawn condition per riprodurre l'effetto solo per l'istigatore controllato localmente.\n- Lo swing va attivato da un notify nel montage dell'ability, lato client predetto, con lo stesso `UAnimNotify_Haptic` e **Can Be Filtered Via Request** disattivato. Il colpo a segno va su una cue applicata dal GameplayEffect di danno.\n- Per resistenze persistenti legate all'arma equipaggiata usa `UInputDeviceSubsystem::ActivateDeviceProperty` / `ActivateDevicePropertyOfClass` con `FActivateDevicePropertyParams`, e conserva il `FInputDevicePropertyHandle` per rimuoverla all'unequip (come fa `LyraWeaponInstance.cpp`).\n- Per esplosioni o impatti nel mondo usa `UGameplayStatics::SpawnForceFeedbackAtLocation` con attenuazione.\n\n## 6. Impostazioni utente e debug\n\n- Opzioni di gioco: usa `APlayerController::bForceFeedbackEnabled` per il toggle e `ForceFeedbackScale` per lo slider di intensità.\n- Debug: `showdebug DeviceProperty` mostra le device property attive, e Rewind Debugger mostra i notify duplicati.\n- La documentazione non riporta una cvar dedicata per spegnere il rumble a livello globale. Usa le proprietà del PlayerController indicate sopra.\n\nDocumentazione: [Force Feedback](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/force-feedback-in-unreal-engine), [Device Properties](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/device-properties-in-unreal-engine), [Motion Matching (Anim Notify Filtering)](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/motion-matching-in-unreal-engine), [Mover](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/mover-in-unreal-engine).\n\nSe mi incolli il notify di foley che usa il tuo AnimBP (nome della classe e campi), ti adatto il codice per agganciare l'aptica lì invece di aggiungere un secondo notify."}

## Q2. How does GASP choose footstep sounds per surface, and what's the simplest way to extend it so sounds (and haptics) change by surface, e.g. with MetaSounds?

{"conversationId":"01M7ZDPV5DNSEQ4F31BE10W867","reply":"The official docs don't describe GASP's foley pipeline, so check the specifics below against your copy. The engine features I build on (Physical Materials, Surface Types, MetaSound parameters, Force Feedback) are documented and linked at the end.\n\n## How stock GASP plays footsteps\n\n- **Trigger:** the locomotion sequences in the Motion Matching databases have foley Anim Notifies on foot plants. In stock GASP the class is `BP_AnimNotify_FoleyEvent`. Each notify has a gameplay tag for the event type (walk, run, land, and so on) and a foot side.\n- **Selection:** the notify gets a foley \"bank\" asset from the owning character and looks up the MetaSound by **event tag**. It then plays that sound at the foot location.\n- **Surface:** in the GASP versions I know, the lookup uses the event tag only. There's no Physical Material or Surface Type branch, so every surface sounds the same. If your fork's notify already does a trace, extend that instead.\n\nIf your `Received_Notify` graph looks different, paste a screenshot or description and I'll adjust this.\n\n## Simplest extension: trace at the notify, then map Surface Type to data\n\nYou don't need to re-author any notifies. Keep GASP's existing notify placements and add a surface lookup inside the notify.\n\n### 1. Surface Types and Physical Materials\n- Go to **Project Settings > Physics > Physical Surface** and name the slots (`Concrete`, `Grass`, `Wood`, `Metal`, and so on). They're saved in `Config/DefaultEngine.ini`.\n- Create a Physical Material per surface, set its **Surface Type**, and assign it to the Materials or Material Instances. For Landscape, assign them per layer.\n\n### 2. MetaSounds: one base graph plus Presets\n- `MSS_Footstep_Base`: a **WaveAsset array** input picked by **Random Get** into a **Wave Player**, plus `Volume` and pitch-randomization inputs.\n- Create a **MetaSound Source Preset** per surface (`MSS_Footstep_Concrete`, `MSS_Footstep_Grass`, and so on) that only overrides the wave array. You maintain one graph, and adding a surface is just data.\n- Optional: add an `Intensity` (float) input to the base graph. Set it at spawn time with **Set Float Parameter** (or `SetFloatParameter` in C++) on the returned Audio Component to separate walk, run, and land.\n\n### 3. Data asset: Surface to Sound and Force Feedback\n\n```cpp\n// FootstepSurfaceSet.h\n#pragma once\n#include \"Engine/DataAsset.h\"\n#include \"Chaos/ChaosEngineInterface.h\"\n#include \"FootstepSurfaceSet.generated.h\"\n\nclass USoundBase;\nclass UForceFeedbackEffect;\n\nUSTRUCT(BlueprintType)\nstruct FFootstepSurfaceEntry\n{\n    GENERATED_BODY()\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, BlueprintReadOnly) TObjectPtr<USoundBase> Sound;            // MetaSound preset\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, BlueprintReadOnly) TObjectPtr<UForceFeedbackEffect> Haptic;  // optional\n};\n\nUCLASS(BlueprintType)\nclass UFootstepSurfaceSet : public UPrimaryDataAsset\n{\n    GENERATED_BODY()\npublic:\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, BlueprintReadOnly) TMap<TEnumAsByte<EPhysicalSurface>, FFootstepSurfaceEntry> Entries;\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, BlueprintReadOnly) FFootstepSurfaceEntry Fallback;\n};\n```\n\n### 4. The notify (C++ version; the Blueprint version has the same steps)\n\n```cpp\n// AnimNotify_SurfaceFootstep.h\nUCLASS(meta=(DisplayName=\"Surface Footstep\"))\nclass UAnimNotify_SurfaceFootstep : public UAnimNotify\n{\n    GENERATED_BODY()\npublic:\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere) FName FootBone = TEXT(\"foot_l\");\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere) TObjectPtr<UFootstepSurfaceSet> SurfaceSet;\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere) float TraceUp = 20.f;\n    UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere) float TraceDown = 50.f;\n\n    virtual void Notify(USkeletalMeshComponent* MeshComp, UAnimSequenceBase* Animation,\n                        const FAnimNotifyEventReference& EventReference) override;\n};\n\n// AnimNotify_SurfaceFootstep.cpp\nvoid UAnimNotify_SurfaceFootstep::Notify(USkeletalMeshComponent* MeshComp, UAnimSequenceBase* Animation,\n                                         const FAnimNotifyEventReference& EventReference)\n{\n    Super::Notify(MeshComp, Animation, EventReference);\n    if (!MeshComp || !SurfaceSet) return;\n    UWorld* World = MeshComp->GetWorld();\n    if (!World || World->GetNetMode() == NM_DedicatedServer) return;\n\n    const FVector Foot = MeshComp->GetSocketLocation(FootBone);\n    FCollisionQueryParams Params(SCENE_QUERY_STAT(FootstepTrace), /*bTraceComplex*/ true, MeshComp->GetOwner());\n    Params.bReturnPhysicalMaterial = true;\n\n    FHitResult Hit;\n    EPhysicalSurface Surface = SurfaceType_Default;\n    if (World->LineTraceSingleByChannel(Hit, Foot + FVector(0, 0, TraceUp), Foot - FVector(0, 0, TraceDown),\n                                        ECC_Visibility, Params))\n    {\n        Surface = UPhysicalMaterial::DetermineSurfaceType(Hit.PhysMaterial.Get());\n    }\n\n    const FFootstepSurfaceEntry* Entry = SurfaceSet->Entries.Find(Surface);\n    if (!Entry) Entry = &SurfaceSet->Fallback;\n\n    if (Entry->Sound)\n    {\n        UGameplayStatics::SpawnSoundAtLocation(World, Entry->Sound, Hit.bBlockingHit ? Hit.ImpactPoint : Foot);\n    }\n\n    // Haptics: local player only\n    if (Entry->Haptic)\n    {\n        if (APawn* Pawn = Cast<APawn>(MeshComp->GetOwner()); Pawn && Pawn->IsLocallyControlled())\n        {\n            if (APlayerController* PC = Cast<APlayerController>(Pawn->GetController()))\n            {\n                PC->ClientPlayForceFeedback(Entry->Haptic);\n            }\n        }\n    }\n}\n```\n\n**Blueprint alternative:** inside `BP_AnimNotify_FoleyEvent`'s `Received_Notify`, use **Get Socket Location** (foot bone), then **Line Trace By Channel** (enable **Trace Complex**), **Break Hit Result**, **Phys Mat**, and **Get Surface Type**. Use the result as the key in the data asset Map to pick the sound and haptic. Then call **Spawn Sound at Location**, and **Client Play Force Feedback** only if the pawn passes **Is Locally Controlled**. Tip: send haptics only for run and land event tags, or the controller buzzes constantly while walking.\n\n## What matters for your setup\n\n| Topic | Recommendation |\n|---|---|\n| **Mover + NP at 62.5 Hz** | Keep footsteps purely cosmetic, triggered by anim notifies. Don't route them through Mover, NP, or GAS. Animation ticks per rendered frame and isn't resimulated on rollback, so you won't get duplicate sounds. Notifies also fire on simulated proxies, so remote players hear footsteps without replication. |\n| **Why trace from the bone instead of using Mover's floor result** | Mover's floor check is centered on the capsule and runs at the sim tick, and the physmat may not be requested. A trace at the foot bone matches the smoothed, rendered pose and handles one foot on a different surface. |\n| **Motion Matching blends** | Several sequences can be in the blend stack, which can cause double steps. On each notify event in the sequences, raise **Trigger Weight Threshold** (for example `0.5`) so only the dominant animation fires. |\n| **Dedicated server** | Leave **Trigger on Dedicated Server** off on the notify events. The code also returns early on `NM_DedicatedServer`. |\n| **Cost / LOD** | Use **Notify Filter Type** = `LOD` with **Notify Filter LOD** to skip traces on distant characters. |\n| **GAS fork** | Don't use GameplayCues for per-step foley: it's too much overhead for something that fires every step. |\n\n**CVars:** this setup doesn't need any.
